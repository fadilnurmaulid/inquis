## 1. Purpose

This document defines INQUIS's strategic, structural, and documentary alignment with the requirements of **Lomba Inovasi Digital Mahasiswa (LIDM) 2026**, specifically the **IPDP (Inovasi Pembelajaran Digital dan Pendidikan)** division. It serves as the translation layer between the competition's evaluation criteria and every product, engineering, and pedagogical decision made in INQUIS.

Every team member must read this document before contributing to the project. Every feature, every screen, every interaction must be evaluable against the criteria described here. When a design decision cannot be justified against this document or `steering/product-vision.md`, it should not be built.

This document also serves as a preparation guide for the presentation, demonstration, and written submission phases of the competition.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 About LIDM

Lomba Inovasi Digital Mahasiswa (LIDM) is a national-level digital innovation competition organized by the Direktorat Jenderal Pendidikan Tinggi, Riset, dan Teknologi (Ditjen Diktiristek) of the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology of Indonesia. It targets undergraduate students across Indonesian universities and evaluates their capacity to produce meaningful digital innovations that address real national challenges.

LIDM 2026 is the most recent iteration of this competition. It encompasses multiple divisions spanning health technology, creative digital media, smart governance, and education.

### 2.2 About Division IPDP

The **IPDP (Inovasi Pembelajaran Digital dan Pendidikan)** division is specifically focused on innovations in digital learning and education. Submissions must:

- Address a genuine and documented educational problem in Indonesia
- Propose a digital solution that is innovative, not merely an incremental improvement
- Demonstrate technical implementation quality
- Show measurable or plausible educational impact
- Align with current national education policy and priorities

### 2.3 Competition Theme 2026

The overarching theme of LIDM 2026 is:

> **"Literasi Digital Terbina, Talenta Indonesia Berdampak Nyata"**  
> *(Nurtured Digital Literacy, Indonesian Talent with Real Impact)*

This theme emphasizes two interconnected imperatives:

1. **Literasi Digital Terbina** — Digital literacy must be actively cultivated, not assumed. Innovations must demonstrate how they build meaningful, critical, and productive relationships between Indonesian learners and digital tools.

2. **Talenta Indonesia Berdampak Nyata** — The goal is measurable real-world impact, not theoretical potential. Submissions are expected to show or credibly project how they improve actual outcomes for Indonesian people.

INQUIS addresses both imperatives directly and simultaneously:

- It builds digital literacy by teaching children to use technology purposefully as an inquiry and exploration tool
- It produces demonstrable impact through embedded scientific thinking assessment that tracks growth in observable cognitive skills

## 3. Educational Objectives (Competition-Specific)

### 3.1 Demonstrate Innovation in Digital Learning Methodology
INQUIS must demonstrate that it is not simply digitizing a worksheet or gamifying a textbook. The innovation claim is specifically:

> The integration of inquiry-based learning methodology with mathematical activities for children aged 5–7 in an interactive digital environment that explicitly scaffolds the development of scientific thinking skills — an approach with no widely deployed equivalent in Indonesian early education.

This claim must be visible in every layer of the product: in the interaction design, in the feedback system, in the assessment architecture, and in the documentation.

### 3.2 Address a Documented National Educational Problem
The documentation must present clear, cited evidence that:

- Indonesian early childhood education is predominantly rote-based
- The cognitive and dispositional outcomes of rote-based early education are suboptimal by national and international standards
- Inquiry-based alternatives exist in the research literature and global practice
- No widely accessible digital implementation of inquiry-based early mathematics learning currently exists in Indonesia

### 3.3 Align with Kurikulum Merdeka
INQUIS must explicitly map its learning activities to the competencies and profile dimensions of Kurikulum Merdeka, particularly the **Profil Pelajar Pancasila**:

| Profil Pelajar Pancasila Dimension | INQUIS Mapping |
|---|---|
| Beriman, Bertakwa, dan Berakhlak Mulia | Character reinforcement through respectful, patient exploration |
| Berkebinekaan Global | Use of diverse, culturally relevant visual contexts in activities |
| Gotong Royong | Future peer learning features; shared discovery framing |
| Mandiri | Self-directed inquiry; child-operated interface |
| Bernalar Kritis | Core outcome: scientific thinking and logical reasoning |
| Kreatif | Open-ended exploration activities; multiple valid approaches |

The most directly targeted dimensions are **Bernalar Kritis (Critical Reasoning)** and **Mandiri (Self-Directed)**, which align precisely with inquiry learning and INQUIS's child-operated design philosophy.

## 4. Product Objectives (Competition-Specific)

### 4.1 Submission-Ready Product
The product must be in a state that can be demonstrated live to evaluators with:
- No critical bugs in the core learning flow
- All four worlds accessible and playable
- Teacher panel functional and data-populated with demo accounts
- Responsive display on tablet viewport

### 4.2 Compelling Demonstration Narrative
The product must support a structured 10–15 minute demonstration that:
1. Opens with the problem statement (30 seconds — shown in the product landing page)
2. Shows the child experience end-to-end for one complete activity (3–4 minutes)
3. Shows the teacher dashboard with populated data (2 minutes)
4. Shows the scientific thinking assessment summary (2 minutes)
5. Explains the pedagogical rationale visually within the product (2 minutes)
6. Closes with the innovation claim and measurable outcomes (2 minutes)

This narrative must be embedded in the product itself, not dependent on an external slide deck.

### 4.3 Written Documentation Quality
All documentation in `.kiro/` must be of sufficient quality that evaluators reading it come away with confidence in the team's depth of understanding. Documentation should demonstrate:
- Command of relevant educational research
- Coherent pedagogical framework
- Professional engineering specification quality
- Deliberate alignment between product decisions and educational outcomes

### 4.4 Differentiation from Competing Submissions
INQUIS must be clearly differentiated from other likely IPDP submissions, which typically include:
- LMS platforms for higher education
- Quiz-based learning tools
- Gamified vocabulary or arithmetic drills
- E-learning portals for specific subjects

INQUIS is differentiated by its target age group (5–7 years), its focus on process skills rather than content knowledge, and its explicit inquiry-based methodology. This differentiation must be visible and articulable within 60 seconds of a judge interacting with the platform.

## 5. User Personas (Competition Context)

### Persona: LIDM Academic Evaluator
**Profile:** University lecturer or researcher in education, educational technology, or computer science. Age 35–55. Likely familiar with constructivist learning theory. May or may not have experience with early childhood education specifically. Will spend approximately 15–30 minutes reviewing the submission including live demonstration and documentation review.

**Evaluation Priorities:**
- Is this genuinely innovative?
- Is the educational rationale sound?
- Is the technical implementation competent?
- Does it plausibly produce the claimed outcomes?
- Does it align with national education priorities?

**Design Implications for Competition:**
- The platform must have a visible "About the Pedagogy" accessible area for evaluators
- Every feature must have an implicit pedagogical explanation visible to someone observing
- The product must feel complete, not prototype-level

### Persona: LIDM Technical Evaluator
**Profile:** IT professional or software engineering academic. Evaluates the technical quality, scalability, and robustness of the digital solution.

**Evaluation Priorities:**
- Is the technology stack appropriate?
- Is the architecture coherent and scalable?
- Is the code maintainable?
- Is the user experience technically smooth?

**Design Implications for Competition:**
- Architecture decisions must be documented and justifiable
- The tech stack (Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Supabase, Vercel) must be presented as deliberate choices with rationale
- No visible runtime errors, layout breaks, or broken interactions during demo

## 7. Functional Requirements (Competition-Specific)

### FR-LIDM-001: Evaluator Landing Experience
The root URL (`/`) must render an evaluator-appropriate landing page that communicates within 30 seconds:
- The name and tagline of the product
- The target population (children aged 5–7)
- The core innovation claim (inquiry-based scientific thinking via mathematics)
- A clear call to action: "Try as Child" / "Try as Teacher" / "Learn More"

### FR-LIDM-002: Demo Mode
A demo mode must exist that populates the teacher dashboard and child progress views with pre-seeded realistic data. This ensures evaluators can see populated states without needing to complete full learning sessions.

Trigger: A "Demo Mode" button on the landing page or teacher login that loads a pre-configured demo account.

### FR-LIDM-003: Pedagogy Explainer
An accessible, in-product section (reachable from teacher panel and landing page) must explain:
- The inquiry learning methodology
- The scientific thinking skill taxonomy (Observe → Question → Predict → Explore → Conclude)
- How each world targets specific skills
- How the assessment system works
- References to supporting educational research

This section must be suitable for reading by an evaluator without prior knowledge of inquiry learning.

### FR-LIDM-004: Kurikulum Merdeka Alignment Display
Within the pedagogy explainer or teacher panel, a visual mapping must show how INQUIS activities correspond to Profil Pelajar Pancasila dimensions.

### FR-LIDM-005: Impact Metrics Display
The teacher dashboard must include a summary view showing aggregated learning progress across all students. This provides evaluators with visible evidence of the platform's capacity for impact measurement.

### FR-LIDM-006: Responsive Demonstration Quality
All primary flows (child play, teacher dashboard, parent view) must be fully functional and visually polished at 1024px (laptop) and 768px (tablet) viewport widths, which are the most likely display formats during live evaluation.

## 8. Non-Functional Requirements (Competition-Specific)

### NFR-LIDM-001: Zero Critical Failures During Demo
All primary user flows must complete without JavaScript errors, broken layouts, or unresponsive interactions during a 30-minute continuous demo session.

### NFR-LIDM-002: Cold Load Performance
The landing page must reach First Contentful Paint within 2 seconds on a standard WiFi connection (simulated 25 Mbps). Activity pages must be fully interactive within 3 seconds.

### NFR-LIDM-003: Demo Data Integrity
Demo mode data must be realistic: multiple students, varied progress states, completed and in-progress activities, meaningful assessment scores. Evaluators must not see all-zero or all-perfect data.

### NFR-LIDM-004: Visual Polish
The product must look intentionally designed. No placeholder colors, temporary fonts, or "lorem ipsum" text may appear in any screen that an evaluator might see.

### NFR-LIDM-005: Documentation Completeness
Every file in `.kiro/steering/` and `.kiro/specs/` must be complete before the submission deadline. Incomplete documents are worse than absent documents — they signal abandonment.

## 9. UX Principles (Competition Context)

### P-LIDM-1: First Impressions are Evaluative Moments
Evaluators will form their primary impression within the first 60 seconds. The landing page, the first child activity, and the first view of the teacher dashboard must each individually communicate quality, purpose, and innovation.

### P-LIDM-2: Show, Don't Tell
The product must demonstrate its pedagogical claims through interaction, not through text assertions. An evaluator who plays one activity should come away understanding inquiry learning better than one who reads three paragraphs about it.

### P-LIDM-3: Documentation as Product
In a competition context, the `.kiro/` documentation is itself a product deliverable. Its quality reflects directly on the team's capability assessment. Write every document as if it will be printed and bound.

### P-LIDM-4: Coherence Across All Layers
Every layer of the product — visual design, interaction model, assessment system, documentation — must tell the same story. Incoherence between layers (e.g., beautiful UI but shallow assessment) creates doubt in evaluators.

## 12. Business Rules (Competition Context)

**BR-LIDM-001:** The demo mode must be accessible without creating an account. It must require no setup from an evaluator.

**BR-LIDM-002:** The product URL must remain stable for the duration of the competition evaluation period. No domain changes, re-deployments that break routes, or authentication changes may occur during this window.

**BR-LIDM-003:** The landing page (`/`) must not redirect to a login page. Evaluators must be able to understand the product before deciding to enter any credentials.

**BR-LIDM-004:** All text in the product that an evaluator might read must be in Bahasa Indonesia (primary) with key technical or pedagogical terms also accessible in English.

**BR-LIDM-005:** The competition submission documentation must be archived and not modified after the submission deadline, even if the product continues development.

---

## 13. Learning Rules (Competition Context)

**LR-LIDM-001:** Every claim made about INQUIS's educational impact must be traceable to a specific feature, interaction, or assessment mechanism. Abstract impact claims without product grounding will not satisfy evaluators.

**LR-LIDM-002:** The inquiry cycle (Observe → Question → Predict → Explore → Conclude) must be visibly present within at least one complete activity that an evaluator can experience during a standard demonstration.

**LR-LIDM-003:** The difference between INQUIS and a quiz-based learning app must be immediately apparent to an evaluator who plays one activity. The absence of a simple right/wrong binary response is the critical differentiator.

## 15. Acceptance Criteria

**AC-LIDM-001:** An evaluator with no prior briefing can understand INQUIS's purpose, target population, and innovation claim from the landing page alone within 30 seconds.

**AC-LIDM-002:** An evaluator can launch demo mode, complete one full child activity, and view the resulting data in the teacher dashboard within 10 minutes.

**AC-LIDM-003:** The pedagogy explainer page provides a complete, accurate, and readable explanation of inquiry learning and its implementation in INQUIS.

**AC-LIDM-004:** A technical evaluator reading the `.kiro/specs/` documents can understand the system architecture, data model, and component design without asking clarifying questions.

**AC-LIDM-005:** The Kurikulum Merdeka and Profil Pelajar Pancasila alignment is displayed clearly enough that a non-specialist evaluator can verify the mapping.

## 16. Edge Cases

**EC-LIDM-001: Evaluator attempts to access child routes without demo mode**  
The system must redirect cleanly to the demo launcher or login page with a clear explanation. No broken or blank screens.

**EC-LIDM-002: Demo mode data is corrupted or missing**  
A seed data recovery script must exist and be executable in under 2 minutes to restore demo data before an evaluation session.

**EC-LIDM-003: Evaluator uses a device with very small or very large screen**  
The product must be functional and non-broken at any viewport between 768px and 1920px wide.

**EC-LIDM-004: Evaluator asks about a feature that is planned but not yet built**  
The product must have a graceful "Coming Soon" placeholder for any planned but unbuilt feature, rather than an error or blank state. This is preferable to removing the reference entirely.

**EC-LIDM-005: Internet connection is slow during demo**  
Critical assets (primary activity content, dashboard data) must be optimized for loading in under 5 seconds on a 10 Mbps connection. A skeleton loading state must prevent blank screens during load.

## 17. Future Expansion (Post-Competition)

Should INQUIS win or place well in LIDM 2026, the following expansion trajectory is envisioned:

- **Phase 2:** Pilot deployment in 3–5 partner schools in East Java
- **Phase 3:** Integration with national school data systems (Dapodik)
- **Phase 4:** Expansion to Worlds 5–7 covering measurement and data inquiry
- **Phase 5:** AI-adaptive scaffolding based on individual learning trajectories
- **Phase 6:** Multi-language support including regional languages
- **Phase 7:** Full offline PWA mode for low-connectivity rural schools

Each phase should be mentioned in competition materials to demonstrate strategic vision beyond the immediate submission.
